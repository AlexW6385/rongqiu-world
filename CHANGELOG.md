# Changelog

What you can see in the world that is new, newest first. Each release follows the encyclopedia written from the novel (`DESIGN.md` §4).

## v2.0 — Places as the book describes them (2026-10-26)

The first pass of checking the world against the book. Seventy of the most important places, plants and creatures of 绒球 now have encyclopedia entries written from the text, and the world was corrected wherever it disagreed with them.

- **The 印加 pit** is now the size the book gives — a rock funnel just over a kilometre across and some four hundred metres deep, with one terrace road spiralling down to a small sandy floor — and it sits deep in the endless desert, where the book puts it.
- **The home tree** is a branchless column fifty metres thick and over three hundred metres tall, moss-green near the top, with a pit and a small lake in its crown. **The frozen giants** on the glacier are its dead kin, frost-grey, scattered a kilometre or two apart.
- **The spire forest** is a dense stand of hundred-metre moss-covered pillars, a forest you can walk across in an afternoon rather than a scatter over a whole region.
- **立蝎之城** stands on an open grass plain: ten fifty-metre pyramids of ochre glued sand in a line, crystal plates catching the sun, and a field of smaller pyramids around them.
- **翡翠龙之城's egg boulders** lie in a tall-fern jungle as the book has them — mossy stones ten to twenty metres high, not hundred-metre towers.
- **银之城 and 创造之城** are walled towns of low silver cone-roofed houses behind metal-bar walls, with barracks, a round hall, and the silver pyramid of the capital. **The eastern continent has no trees**: its jungle is giant succulents — columns, flowering barrels and coral shapes up to fifty metres.
- **龙栖木** are seventy-metre trees with ten-metre trunks, cocoon houses and purple fruit.
- **The floating islands** hang two to three hundred metres up; **the great rift** is ten kilometres wide; **the spiny explosive balls** grow only on the island west of 骷髅岛.
- **Creatures:** 骸龙 are lanky two-metre animals; 毛玉 of this era are only a few centimetres long and run in swarms; the crystal tortoise is twenty metres long; mature 飞龙 are twelve-metre, grey-armoured six-limbed dragons; 亚龙 are black; the great sky jellyfish are dark blue; the 深沙巴士 has black diamond scales and six eyes.
- The page now runs on WebGPU only, and says so plainly on browsers that cannot use it.

See `CONFORMANCE.md` for every change and for what is still to be corrected.

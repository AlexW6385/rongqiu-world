# Changelog

What you can see in the world that is new, newest first. Each release follows the encyclopedia written from the novel (`DESIGN.md` §4).

## v3.0 — Nature as the book places it (2026-10-27)

The end of the nature phase: plants, landscapes and creatures are now spread the way the encyclopedia entries describe them.

- **The spire forest** is what the book describes: hundred-metre moss-green pillars packed in rows with gaps a few metres wide, so dense that little light reaches the ground, ringed by a wall of the tallest pillars and standing on open rock.
- **The floating archipelago** is a dozen forested islands and hundreds of bare song-stone chunks drifting two to three hundred metres over the jungle.
- **骷髅岛** is a flat island some twenty kilometres long in its fog sea, set with a thousand click-eggs in their rocks, whose double clicks you hear before you see anything.
- **立蝎 pyramids** stand alone and in short rows in clearings all through the southern jungle; **the blood-ball trees** make a red belt round the east's desert, with the moss plain beyond; **the spiny explosive balls** grow in one line where their island's moss ends.
- **Herds and rarities**: horned dinosaurs in herds of a few dozen, 骸龙 in bands, 迷雾蠕虫 in threes; one crystal tortoise to a region; the last 飞龙 only by the volcanoes.

## v2.1 — Nature, first round (2026-10-27)

- **The underworld** under the endless desert: a cavern kilometres wide whose ceiling glows like a sun and dims at night, a forest of fern trees, home trees standing like pillars, and the 死亡尖刺 wall at its edge. Enter it from the map.
- **The east** lies under three layers of cloud a hundred or two metres above the ground, and in its middle stands **the fog pillar** — a white spiral column fifty kilometres wide rising through the clouds, seen from hundreds of kilometres away (a new map place).
- **云中山峰** is a steep rock mountain rising through all three cloud layers; sixty kilometres away the lower **Academy peak** just clears the top layer, with the 白骸龙's silver halls on its summit (both on the map).
- **The home tree** is wrapped at a hundred metres in a cloud of floating fluff-ball creatures, and a waterfall pours from a hole high on its trunk.
- **The moon** is the moon of a visit: sky-blue and larger than the sun, a sea world with pale islands.
- **The twisted jungle** has hundred-metre giants along its desert edge and a dark second layer of crossing trunks; **the volcano** rises over a kilometre from its middle with a crater at the top.
- **The great rift** is filled with fog nearly to its rim; walk into **the fog seas** round 骷髅岛 and visibility drops to a few dozen metres.
- **The canyon sea** freezes into a glacier in the north, where the dead giant trees stand; **the crystal ring** rises out of the polar ice; **the south** is an empty ice continent.
- **The crystal woodland**: leafy trees with pale glassy bark, spaced apart on bare grey gravel.
- **翡翠港** stands on its shore; the peoples of the book — 翡翠龙, 白骸龙, 银甲龙, 立蝎, 脑袋虫 with their mounds, 阿兹特克虫, 印加 wasps — and pterosaurs of four sizes live round their cities.
- **The sea**: turquoise shallows where the sand shows through, the sky reflected by angle, surf breaking on the beaches.
- **Skies by region**: clear over the desert and the salt flats, cloudier over the jungle, the east and the fog seas.
- **Sound**: wind that rises with height, leaves in the forests, surf, sand hiss, the hum and drips of the cave, insects at night.

## v2.0 — Places as the book describes them (2026-10-26)

The first pass of checking the world against the book. Seventy of the most important places, plants and creatures of 绒球 now have encyclopedia entries written from the text, and the world was corrected wherever it disagreed with them.

- **The 印加 pit** is now the size the book gives — a rock funnel just over a kilometre across and some four hundred metres deep, with one terrace road spiralling down to a small sandy floor — and it sits deep in the endless desert, where the book puts it.
- **The home tree** is a branchless column fifty metres thick and over three hundred metres tall, moss-green near the top, with a pit and a small lake in its crown. **The frozen giants** on the glacier are its dead kin, frost-grey, scattered a kilometre or two apart.
- **The spire forest** is a dense stand of hundred-metre moss-covered pillars, a forest you can walk across in an afternoon rather than a scatter over a whole region.
- **立蝎之城** stands on an open grass plain: ten fifty-metre pyramids of ochre glued sand in a line, crystal plates catching the sun, and a field of smaller pyramids around them.
- **翡翠龙之城's egg boulders** lie in a tall-fern jungle as the book has them — mossy stones ten to twenty metres high, not hundred-metre towers.
- **银之城 and 创造之城** are walled towns of low silver cone-roofed houses behind metal-bar walls, with barracks, a round hall, and the silver pyramid of the capital. **The eastern continent has no trees**: its jungle is giant succulents — columns, flowering barrels and coral shapes up to fifty metres.
- **龙栖木** are seventy-metre trees with ten-metre trunks, cocoon houses and purple fruit.
- **The floating islands** hang two to three hundred metres up; **the great rift** is ten kilometres wide; **the spiny explosive balls** grow only on the island west of 骷髅岛.
- **Creatures:** 骸龙 are lanky two-metre animals; 毛玉 of this era are only a few centimetres long and run in swarms; the crystal tortoise is twenty metres long; mature 飞龙 are twelve-metre, grey-armoured six-limbed dragons; 亚龙 are black; the great sky jellyfish are dark blue; the 深沙巴士 has black diamond scales and six eyes.
